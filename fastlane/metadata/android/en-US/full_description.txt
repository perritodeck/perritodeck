Perrito turns an Android tablet or phone into a grid of buttons that control your Windows, macOS or Linux computer. Mute your mic, switch OBS scenes, fire keyboard shortcuts, launch apps, play and pause music, or trigger your smart home — all with one tap.

<b>How it works</b>
Install the free PerritoDeck Host on your computer, open Perrito on your tablet, and connect over Wi-Fi or a USB cable. You design the buttons on your computer; the tablet shows them instantly and live-updates titles and icons.

<b>Give an old tablet a new job</b>
Perrito runs on Android 5.1 and newer, including Amazon Fire tablets. That tablet in the drawer has a bigger, sharper screen than most dedicated macro pads.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud
• No ads, analytics or tracking
• The tablet talks only to the computer you connect it to

<b>Features</b>
• Full-screen button grid sized to your screen
• Press several buttons at once, with haptic feedback
• Live titles and icons pushed from your computer
• Reconnects automatically if Wi-Fi drops
• Works over USB (adb reverse) when Wi-Fi isn't an option
