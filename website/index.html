Your old tablet is a control deck now.

PerritoDeck turns any Android tablet or phone into a grid of buttons for your computer. Mute your mic, switch scenes, launch apps, fire hotkeys, control your smart home. Free and open source, with no account and no cloud.

Get PerritoDeck View the source

Go on, press a key.

Set up in about two minutes

  1. Install the host

    Run PerritoDeck Host on your Windows, Mac or Linux computer. It sits in the background and does what your buttons ask.

  2. Install Perrito on the tablet

    Any Android tablet or phone from the last decade works, including Amazon Fire tablets. It will be on F-Droid, Google Play and GitHub.

  3. Pair and start pressing

    Scan the code on your computer's screen, or plug in a USB cable. Lay out your buttons on the computer and they appear on the tablet instantly.

Why it's different

Hardware you already own

That tablet in the drawer has a better screen than most macro pads. Give it a second job instead of sending it to a landfill.

Nothing leaves your desk

The tablet talks only to your computer, over your own Wi-Fi or a USB cable. No sign-up, no tracking, no subscription.

Linux is a first-class citizen

The host runs the same on Windows, macOS and Linux, including Wayland desktops.

Open all the way down

Apache-licensed app, MIT-licensed plugin SDK, and a documented protocol so anyone can build their own client or integration.
