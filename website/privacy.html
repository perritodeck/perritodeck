PerritoDeck

Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

This policy covers the Perrito apps for Android, iPhone and iPad and the PerritoDeck desktop app. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. Neither app has analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or user accounts, and neither sends data to us or to any third party.

What the apps store on your devices

  • Perrito (Android) stores the address of the computer you connect to, a random device ID it creates on first launch, and the pairing secret for that computer. These stay in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall it.
  • PerritoDeck Host stores your button layouts, the list of paired tablets and its own security certificate in your computer's settings folder.

Network connections

The Android app connects only to the computer you choose, over your local network or a USB cable. It sends that computer your device's name, model, Android version and screen size so the host can size the buttons, plus which buttons you press. The host does not forward any of this anywhere.

The host contacts the internet only when you ask it to: "Look online" on the Plugins page downloads the plugin list from perritodeck.dev, and the optional update check (off unless you turn it on) asks GitHub for the latest version number. Neither sends anything about you or your setup.

Actions you set up yourself may contact other services — for example, a button that opens a website or controls your smart-home hub. Those connections are made by your computer to services you chose, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

The Android and iOS apps request network access to reach your computer, and camera access to scan pairing QR codes. The camera is only used while the scanner is open, and nothing it sees is stored or sent anywhere.

Children

The apps collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The full history is public in the project's source repository.
